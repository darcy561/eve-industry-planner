# Price History Dialogue

The Price History dialogue displays historical price trends for items, showing how prices have changed over time in selected market regions.

## Overview

The Price History dialogue provides:
- Historical price charts
- Price trends over time
- Regional price comparisons
- Visual price analysis

## Key Features

### Price Charts

- Interactive line graphs
- Historical price data
- Multiple time periods
- Visual trend analysis

### Regional Comparison

- Compare prices across regions
- Switch between regions
- View regional price differences
- Analyze market variations

### Time Periods

- View different time ranges
- Analyze short and long-term trends
- Identify price patterns
- Plan based on historical data

### Data Visualization

- Clear chart presentation
- Easy-to-read graphs
- Interactive controls
- Export capabilities

## Usage

1. Click on an item in a job or elsewhere
2. Select "View Price History" or similar option
3. Choose market region
4. Review historical price trends
5. Use data for pricing decisions
