# Price Entry Dialogue

The Price Entry dialogue allows you to quickly enter material costs for one or more jobs, streamlining the cost tracking process.

## Overview

The Price Entry dialogue provides:
- Quick cost entry for multiple materials
- Market data integration
- Bulk cost updates
- Cost distribution across jobs

## Key Features

### Material Cost Entry

- Enter costs for individual materials
- Set costs for multiple materials at once
- Import costs from market data
- Import costs from clipboard (multibuy format)

### Market Integration

- Select market location
- Choose market order type (buy/sell orders)
- Import prices automatically
- Update prices from market data

### Bulk Operations

- Enter costs for multiple jobs simultaneously
- Distribute costs across related jobs
- Apply costs to selected materials
- Batch update functionality

### Cost Distribution

- Distribute item costs between jobs
- Handle shared material costs
- Calculate per-job costs
- Track cost allocation

## Usage

1. Select jobs or materials that need cost entry
2. Open the Price Entry dialogue
3. Choose market location and order type (optional)
4. Enter costs manually or import from market
5. Apply costs to selected jobs
