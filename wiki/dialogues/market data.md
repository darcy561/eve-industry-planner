# Market Data Dialogue

The Market Data dialogue displays current market prices, buy orders, and sell orders for items at selected market locations.

## Overview

The Market Data dialogue provides:
- Current market prices for items
- Buy and sell order information
- Market location selection
- Price comparison across locations

## Key Features

### Market Information

- View current market prices
- See buy order prices
- See sell order prices
- Compare prices across locations

### Location Selection

- Select market hub or region
- Change location to compare prices
- View location-specific data
- Filter by market type

### Order Details

- View order quantities
- See order prices
- Check order age
- Monitor market activity

### Data Grid Display

- Organised data presentation
- Sortable columns
- Filterable data
- Export capabilities

## Usage

1. Click on an item in a job or elsewhere
2. Select "View Market Data" or similar option
3. Choose market location
4. Review prices and orders
5. Use data for cost entry or planning
