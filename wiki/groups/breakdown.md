# Group — Breakdown tab

The Breakdown tab summarises costs and scale for the whole group, then splits detail by output job (top-level products with no parent jobs in the chain sense used elsewhere on the group page).

Use it when you need project-level numbers—total spend, install and extras, invention—before selling or when comparing multiple finished products in one build.

## Group totals

At the top, the tab shows aggregates across all jobs in the group:

| Metric | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Total jobs | Count of every included planner job |
| Total output jobs | Jobs with no parent jobs (finished products of the project) |
| Total raw material purchase cost | Sum of recorded material purchase costs on member jobs |
| Total install costs | Combined industry install costs |
| Total extras costs | Combined extras (fees, surcharges, etc.) |
| Total invention costs | Combined invention-related costs where applicable |
| Total involved characters | Characters from ESI industry jobs linked to each planner job in the group (unique per job, then summed for the group total) |

Figures update from the current job data in memory; open [Edit Job](../../edit%20job) on individual runs if you need to change underlying costs.

## Per output job sections

Below the totals, one card per output job shows:

- Item icon and name
- The same cost categories, but only for jobs in that output’s related chain (the output itself plus every job linked as supplier/consumer in the group graph for that product)
- A list of related jobs contributing to those numbers

Each output card is colour-accented by job type (manufacturing or reaction) for quick scanning.

## When to use it

- Compare two or more finished products in the same group (e.g. hull plus modules sold separately).
- Sanity-check total project cost before marking output jobs Ready For Sale on the main planner.
- See how many characters appear on linked ESI industry jobs across the chain.
