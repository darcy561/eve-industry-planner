# Job Planner

The Job Planner is the central hub for managing your industry jobs. It organises your jobs into five stages, with each material represented as a job card that progresses through the workflow as you build materials.

## Overview

The Job Planner displays all your industry jobs organised by their current status:
- **Planning** - Jobs in the planning phase
- **Purchasing** - Jobs where materials are being acquired
- **Building** - Active manufacturing jobs
- **Complete** - Finished jobs ready for sale
- **Selling** - Jobs with active sell orders

## Key Features

### Job Cards

Each job is displayed as a card showing:
- Item name and icon
- Current status/stage
- Material requirements
- Progress information
- Cost and profit data

### Multi-Select Actions

Use the checkbox in the corner of job cards to:
- Select single or multiple jobs
- Apply bulk actions from the options menu
- Quickly manage multiple jobs at once

### Search and Filter

- Use the search bar to find specific jobs
- Filter jobs by status, item type, or other criteria
- Navigate through large job lists efficiently

## Getting Started

1. **Add New Jobs**: Click "Add New Job" from the left menu to create new industry jobs
2. **Select Jobs**: Use checkboxes to select jobs for bulk operations
3. **Manage Jobs**: Use the side menu options to perform actions on selected jobs
