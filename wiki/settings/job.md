# Job Settings

The Job Settings section allows you to configure default values and preferences that will be applied to new industry jobs and job planning.

## Default Market Settings

### Default Market Hub

**Dropdown:** Select your preferred market hub

This setting determines which market hub will be used by default when:
- Creating new industry jobs
- Calculating material costs
- Viewing market prices

Common market hubs include:
- Jita IV - Moon 4 - Caldari Navy Assembly Plant (The Forge)
- Amarr VIII (Oris) - Emperor Family Academy (Domain)
- Dodixie IX - Moon 20 - Federation Navy Assembly Plant (Sinq Laison)
- Rens VI - Moon 8 - Brutor Tribe Treasury (Heimatar)
- Hek VIII - Moon 12 - Boundless Creation Factory (Metropolis)

### Default Market Orders

**Dropdown:** Select your preferred market order type

Choose whether to use buy orders, sell orders, or a specific order type by default when calculating material costs and market prices.

## Job Display Options

### Hide Complete Materials

**Toggle:** Enable/Disable hiding complete materials

When enabled, materials that have been fully acquired will be hidden from the job materials list, making it easier to focus on items you still need to obtain.

- **Enabled:** Completed materials are hidden
- **Disabled:** All materials are shown regardless of completion status

## Asset Location Settings

### Default Asset Location

**Dropdown:** Select your default asset location

This setting allows you to choose a default location from your character's assets. This location will be used when:
- Creating new jobs
- Calculating material availability
- Planning material transport

The dropdown will populate with all locations where you have assets stored, including:
- Stations
- Structures (Citadels, Engineering Complexes, etc.)
- Ships

## Citadel Settings

### Citadel Brokers Fee Percentage

**Number Input:** Enter the broker's fee percentage for citadels

Set the default broker's fee percentage that applies to transactions in player-owned structures (citadels). This value is used when calculating costs for jobs that will be manufactured in custom structures.

- Enter a decimal value (e.g., `3.5` for 3.5%)
- The value is automatically rounded to 2 decimal places
- This setting affects cost calculations for jobs in custom structures

## Advanced Job Settings

### Custom System Indexes

The Job Settings page also includes options for custom system indexes, which allow you to configure additional parameters for job calculations.

### Custom Extras

Additional custom settings and preferences can be configured in the Custom Extras section.

## How Settings Are Saved

All job settings are automatically saved to your account with a 2-second debounce delay. Changes are synced across all your devices.
