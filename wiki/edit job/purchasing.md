# Edit Job - Purchasing Stage

The Purchasing stage is where you track material acquisition and record costs for all materials needed to build your job. This stage bridges the gap between planning and production, ensuring you have all required materials before beginning manufacturing.

## Stage Purpose

The Purchasing stage serves to:
- **Track material acquisition** by monitoring which materials have been acquired and which are still needed
- **Record material costs** by entering purchase prices and importing costs from various sources
- **Manage child job relationships** by linking jobs that produce materials needed for this job
- **Monitor progress** by tracking completion status and total costs
- **Prepare for production** by ensuring all materials are ready before moving to the building stage

## Lifecycle Position

The Purchasing stage appears after planning in the job lifecycle:

1. [Planning](planning) - Configuring job parameters and reviewing requirements
2. **Purchasing** ← You are here
3. [Building](building) - Active manufacturing and production
4. [Complete](complete) - Finished production ready for sale
5. [Selling](selling) - Managing sales and market orders

After completing the purchasing stage (all materials acquired), jobs move to the Building stage where manufacturing begins.

## Purchasing Panels

The Purchasing stage consists of the following panels:

### [Purchasing Data Panel](purchasing/purchasing%20data%20panel)
Overview panel showing material acquisition progress, total costs, and providing quick actions for shopping lists and cost import.

### [Material Cards](purchasing/material%20cards)
Individual cards for each material showing quantities, costs, child job relationships, and completion status with cost entry forms.

### [Invention Costs Card](purchasing/invention%20costs%20card)
Track additional invention-related costs for T2 items, including datacores, decryptors, and other invention materials.

### [Job Setup Info](purchasing/job%20setup%20info)
Horizontal scrollable view of all job setups showing configuration details, production quantities, and structure information.

### [Child Job Dialogue](purchasing/child%20job%20dialogue)
Modal dialogue for managing child job relationships, allowing you to link existing jobs or create new ones for materials you're producing.
