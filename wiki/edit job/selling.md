# Edit Job - Selling Stage

The Selling stage is where you manage market orders, track sales transactions, and analyse profitability for completed jobs. This stage provides comprehensive tools for linking market orders, recording sales, and calculating final profit/loss.

## Stage Purpose

The Selling stage serves to:
- **Link market orders** by connecting your job to active sell orders from EVE Online
- **Track sales** by linking transactions from the ESI API or manually entering sales data
- **Calculate profitability** by comparing total costs against sales revenue
- **Monitor order status** by tracking order progress, completion, and cancellation
- **Analyse performance** by reviewing sales statistics, average prices, and profit margins

## Lifecycle Position

The Selling stage appears after completion in the job lifecycle:

1. [Planning](planning) - Configuring job parameters and reviewing requirements
2. [Purchasing](purchasing) - Acquiring materials and recording costs
3. [Building](building) - Active manufacturing and production
4. [Complete](complete) - Finished production ready for sale
5. **Selling** ← You are here

This is the final stage in the job lifecycle, where you track sales and analyse profitability before archiving the job.

## Selling Panels

The Selling stage consists of the following panels:

### [Market Costs Panel](selling/market%20costs%20panel)
Displays current market prices across different market options (buy/sell) for the produced item, with quick access to market data and history.

### [Market Order Panel](selling/market%20order%20panel)
Manages market order linking with two tabs: Available Orders (orders that can be linked) and Linked Orders (currently connected orders with status tracking).

### [Sales Stats Panel](selling/sales%20stats%20panel)
Comprehensive sales statistics including total build costs, brokers fees, transaction fees, total sales, average sale price, and profit/loss calculations.

### [Available Transactions Panel](selling/available%20transactions%20panel)
Lists new transactions from ESI API that match your market orders, allowing you to link sales transactions to track actual revenue.

### [Linked Transaction Panel](selling/linked%20transaction%20panel)
Displays all transactions linked to the job, allows manual transaction entry, and provides transaction management for accurate sales tracking.

### [Button Panel](selling/button%20panel)
Provides action buttons for archiving jobs after sales are complete.
