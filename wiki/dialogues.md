# Dialogues

Dialogues are pop-up windows that provide additional functionality and information throughout the application. They can be opened from various locations to perform specific tasks or view detailed data.

## Available Dialogues

- **[Shopping List](dialogues/shopping%20list)** - Generate and manage shopping lists for materials
- **[Price Entry](dialogues/price%20entry)** - Enter material costs for jobs
- **[Assets](dialogues/assets)** - View and select assets from your EVE Online characters
- **[Market Data](dialogues/market%20data)** - View current market prices and orders
- **[Price History](dialogues/price%20history)** - View historical price trends
- **[Import Fit](dialogues/import%20fit)** - Import ship fittings from clipboard

## How Dialogues Work

Dialogues are typically opened:
- From buttons or menu options in various pages
- Through event system triggers
- When specific actions require additional input
- To display detailed information

Most dialogues can be closed by clicking outside the dialogue or using the close button.
