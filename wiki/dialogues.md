# Dialogues

Dialogues are pop-up windows for focused tasks—shopping lists, price entry, market lookups, and similar—without leaving the page you are on.

## Available dialogues

- [Shopping List](dialogues/shopping%20list) - Generate and manage shopping lists for materials
- [Price Entry](dialogues/price%20entry) - Enter material costs for planner jobs
- [Assets](dialogues/assets) - View and select assets from your EVE Online characters
- [Market Data](dialogues/market%20data) - View current market prices and orders
- [Price History](dialogues/price%20history) - View historical price trends
- [Import Fit](dialogues/import%20fit) - Import ship fittings from clipboard

## How dialogues open

You will see them when you use menu actions or buttons on the [Job Planner](job%20planner), [Edit Job](edit%20job), or related screens—for example Shopping List from the planner menu, or Market Data from a material row.

Close a dialogue with its close control or by clicking outside it (where the app allows).
